# Welcome to the Garden

Hello, and thanks for stopping by! This blog is a **digital garden**: a place where ideas are planted early, watered often and *sometimes* harvested. Everything here is written in Obsidian and published to Leaflet with a single command.

%% Private note: remember to update the "About" section in December. %%

## What to expect

I write about three things, roughly in this order of enthusiasm:

1. Bread, mostly [[A Week of Sourdough|sourdough]]
2. Tools for thinking, see [[Notes on Note-Taking]]
3. The occasional deep dive, like the [[Field Guide to Math and Code]]

You can also browse the [[Reading List]] or flip through the [[Sketchbook]].

> [!tip] New here?
> Start with the sourdough diary. It is short, a little messy and very honest.

## How it is made

Each post is a plain Markdown note. Formatting such as ==highlights== (in ==🟢color== too), ~~mistakes~~, <u>underlines</u>, `inline code` and [external links](https://leaflet.pub) is converted to Leaflet rich text.[^stack] Footnotes work too, even inline ones.^[Like this one, written directly in the sentence.]
