I finally did it: a starter, a schedule and seven days of flour everywhere. Here is the diary, with every mistake left in.

## The plan

- [x] Buy rye flour
- [x] Find a jar with a loose lid
- [ ] Name the starter
	- Ideas: *Breadley Cooper*, *Doughlores*, *Clint Yeastwood*
- [ ] Bake something edible

## The schedule

1. Mix 50 g flour and 50 g water
2. Wait 24 hours, then discard half
3. Feed again with 50 g flour and 50 g water
	- Use lukewarm water if the kitchen is cold
	- Mark the level with a rubber band
4. Repeat until the starter doubles in 4 to 6 hours

Somewhere around day five things got interesting:

5. Bubbles everywhere
6. A smell somewhere between yogurt and green apples

## Day 7: the loaf

![[crumb.jpg|The crumb of the first loaf|480]]

Not bad for a first try! The crust was thick, the crumb a bit tight. Next time, a longer bulk fermentation.

> [!warning] Lesson learned
> Never put a closed jar of active starter in a warm oven. **Never.**
